Northstar Meridian Group Employee Handbook (Summary)
Version: 2026.3
Owner: People Operations

1. Employment Terms
- Employment status categories: full-time, part-time, contractor, intern.
- Probation period is 90 days unless local law specifies otherwise.
- Background verification is completed before system access is granted.

2. Working Hours and Attendance
- Core collaboration hours are 10:00-15:00 local time.
- Flexible schedules are supported with manager approval.
- Attendance exceptions must be logged in the HR portal within 24 hours.

3. Performance Management
- Goal setting occurs quarterly.
- Mid-cycle review focuses on progress and risk blockers.
- Annual review calibrates role impact, behaviors, and growth readiness.

4. Leave and Wellbeing
- PTO accrues monthly.
- Sick leave follows local labor requirements and company policy.
- Wellbeing stipend is available for approved health and learning expenses.

5. Conduct and Compliance
- Respectful workplace behavior is mandatory.
- Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation are prohibited.
- Employees must complete compliance training annually.

6. Security and Data Handling
- Corporate data must remain in approved systems.
- Sharing customer data externally requires legal and security approval.
- Lost devices or suspicious emails must be reported immediately.
