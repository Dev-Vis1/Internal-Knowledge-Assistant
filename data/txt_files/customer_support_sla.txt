Customer Support SLA and Escalation Matrix
Owner: Customer Success Operations

Support Tiers
- Tier 1: General usage issues and account questions.
- Tier 2: Product defects and reproducible incidents.
- Tier 3: Engineering-led investigation for systemic or complex failures.

Severity Definitions
- Sev 1: Full outage or data unavailability in production.
- Sev 2: Major feature degradation with workaround unavailable.
- Sev 3: Non-critical feature issue with workaround available.
- Sev 4: Cosmetic issue or enhancement request.

Response Targets
- Sev 1: Initial response within 15 minutes, updates every 30 minutes.
- Sev 2: Initial response within 1 hour, updates every 2 hours.
- Sev 3: Initial response within 4 business hours.
- Sev 4: Initial response within 2 business days.

Escalation
- Sev 1 automatically notifies Incident Commander, Security, and Product Leadership.
- Regional customer impact requires local communications owner assignment.
- Executive summary sent within 24 hours after resolution.
