Northstar Meridian Group Benefits Portal

The benefits portal provides access to healthcare enrollment, retirement plans, wellbeing resources, and leave tools.

Enrollment Windows

  • New hire enrollment: within 30 days of start date
  • Annual open enrollment: November 1 to November 20
  • Life event changes: within 31 days of the event

Supported Programs

  • Medical, dental, and vision plans
  • Retirement contribution matching
  • Mental health and wellness coaching
  • Learning stipend reimbursement
