Security Awareness Program

All workers complete onboarding and annual refresher training in secure behavior, phishing detection, and incident reporting.

Program Components

  1. Quarterly phishing simulation campaigns
  2. Role-based training for engineering, finance, and HR
  3. Secure coding micro-courses for product teams
  4. Mandatory policy attestation at year end

Reporting

Suspected phishing or unauthorized access must be reported through the incident channel or security hotline immediately.
